# LAB 01-05 — Managed Identity

## Business Scenario
An AI workload must access Azure services without storing long-lived credentials.

## Mission
Explore a managed identity and its authorization boundary.

## BUILD
Enable or inspect a managed identity on a supported training resource.

## VALIDATE
Verify identity presence and its intended access scope.

## BREAK
Remove or narrow the required role assignment in the controlled environment.

## FIX
Diagnose identity versus authorization and restore least-privilege access.

## CHALLENGE
Design a secretless access path for a small AI workload.

## CLEANUP
Remove training identity assignments and resources.
