## Operational loop

```text
DEPLOY
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
GOVERN
 ↓
MEASURE COST
 ↓
OPTIMIZE
 ↓
VALIDATE
```

Learners must connect infrastructure decisions to operational evidence: metrics/logs, policy state, tags, cost allocation and optimization changes.
