# Course 10 — Enterprise Agentic AI Platform — Capstone

## Mission
Design and implement an enterprise AI platform from requirements to final engineering defense.

## Requirements
The solution must address Azure, Terraform, Microsoft Foundry, Agent, Search, Storage, Key Vault, Managed Identity, RBAC, monitoring, CI/CD, FinOps and documentation.

## Constraints
- no hard-coded secrets;
- no manual production deployment;
- reusable Terraform modules;
- environment separation;
- explicit security model;
- validation evidence;
- operations model;
- FinOps model.

## Engineering stages
1. Requirements
2. Architecture
3. Threat Model
4. ADRs
5. Terraform Platform
6. Foundry AI workload
7. Security
8. CI/CD
9. Observability
10. FinOps
11. Break/Fix
12. Final Defense

## Deliverables
- Terraform repository
- architecture diagram
- requirements
- ADRs
- security design
- CI/CD
- monitoring
- FinOps
- validation evidence
- troubleshooting report
- final presentation

## Definition of Done
The learner can defend every major architecture and engineering decision with evidence.
