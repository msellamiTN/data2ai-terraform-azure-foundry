# Course 10 Capstone Execution

## Phase 1 — Discover
Extract business, security, data, reliability, governance and cost requirements.

## Phase 2 — Architect
Produce context diagram, trust boundaries, control/knowledge/security/observability planes and ADRs.

## Phase 3 — Build
Implement infrastructure, platform, agent, tools and knowledge plane.

## Phase 4 — Validate
Run infrastructure, security, functional and AI evaluation checks.

## Phase 5 — Break
Instructor introduces controlled failures without revealing the root cause.

## Phase 6 — Recover
Follow the evidence chain and prove remediation.

## Phase 7 — Defend
Present architecture, trade-offs, threat model, operational model, costs and recovery strategy.
